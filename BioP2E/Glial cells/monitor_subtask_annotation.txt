Monitor Condition: Mouse brain tissue (2 g), Hanks solution, and a culture dish are available.
Subtask 1: Use ophthalmic forceps to transfer the brain tissue from the current culture dish into a new sterile culture dish.
Subtask 2: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of Hanks solution and add it to the culture dish.
Subtask 3: Gently shake the culture dish to allow the Hanks solution to fully contact the brain tissue.
Subtask 4: Use a pipette to aspirate the Hanks solution from the culture dish and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 5: Repeat Subtasks 2–4 three times in total.

Monitor Condition: The brain tissue has been washed three times with Hanks solution. A test tube and Hanks solution are available.
Subtask 6: Use ophthalmic forceps to transfer the brain tissue into the test tube.
Subtask 7: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 80 mL of Hanks solution and add it to the test tube.
Subtask 8: Insert the pipette tip 1–2 mm below the liquid surface in the test tube and gently pipette 3–5 times to form a homogeneous suspension.

Monitor Condition: The suspension has been allowed to stand for 10 min.
Subtask 9: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the test tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 10: Repeat Subtasks 7–9 three times in total.

Monitor Condition: Three sedimentation washes have been completed. DMEM/F12 culture medium, a 73 μm nylon screen, and a centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 11: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM/F12 culture medium and add it to the test tube.
Subtask 12: Install the 73 μm nylon screen on the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 13: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the test tube.
Subtask 14: Move the pipette above the 73 μm nylon screen and slowly press the plunger to dispense the cell suspension dropwise onto the nylon screen for filtration.

Monitor Condition: Most of the cell suspension in the test tube has been filtered. DMEM/F12 culture medium is available.
Subtask 15: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM/F12 culture medium.
Subtask 16: Move the pipette above the 73 μm nylon screen and slowly press the plunger to rinse the inner surface of the screen.

Monitor Condition: All the filtrate has been collected in the centrifuge tube. Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 17: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 18: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 19: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 20: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the viable cell count and the target concentration of 1 × 10^4 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. DMEM/F12 culture medium and a culture flask are available.
Subtask 21: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of DMEM/F12 culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to 1 × 10^4 cells/mL.
Subtask 22: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of the diluted cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the culture flask.
Subtask 23: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 24: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 25: Start the incubator.
