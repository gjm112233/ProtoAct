1. In Step (1), “wash three times with Hanks solution” should include the following operations: add Hanks solution, gently shake the culture dish, and remove the solution. After one complete washing cycle, an additional subtask should specify that the corresponding subtasks are repeated until a total of three washes has been completed.
2. In Step (1), the volume of Hanks solution added to the test tube should be approximately 40 times the amount of brain tissue. Since 2 g of brain tissue is used, 80 mL of Hanks solution should be added.
3. In Step (1), “repeat this process three times” should include the following operations: add 80 mL of Hanks solution to the test tube, resuspend the tissue, allow it to stand for 10 min, and discard the supernatant. After one complete cycle, an additional subtask should specify that the corresponding subtasks are repeated until a total of three cycles has been completed.
4. After the filtration operation in Step (2), an operation for rinsing the nylon screen must be included.
