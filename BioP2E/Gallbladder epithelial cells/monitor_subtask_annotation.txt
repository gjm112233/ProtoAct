Monitor Condition: The mucosal surface of the rabbit gallbladder has been thoroughly cleaned. A sterile culture dish and 0.125% type IV collagenase solution are available.
Subtask 1: Transfer the washed gallbladder into a sterile culture dish with the mucosal surface facing downward and attached to the bottom of the dish.
Subtask 2: Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of 0.125% type IV collagenase solution and add it to the culture dish.
Subtask 3: Place the culture dish securely inside the incubator.
Subtask 4: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 5: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The culture dish has been incubated for 15 min. DMEM/F12 culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum is available.
Subtask 6: Remove the culture dish from the incubator and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 7: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM/F12 culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and add it to the culture dish.

Monitor Condition: A centrifuge tube is available.
Subtask 8: Use a pipette to transfer all the cell suspension from the culture dish into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 9: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 10: Set the centrifuge to 1000 rpm for 5 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 11: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 5-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 12: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 13: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 14: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 15: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 16: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 17: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the viable cell count and the target concentration of 5 × 10^4 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. DMEM/F12 culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and a culture flask are available.
Subtask 18: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of DMEM/F12 culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 19: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the culture flask.
Subtask 20: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator.
Subtask 21: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 22: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 3 days. DMEM/F12 culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum is available.
Subtask 23: Remove the culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 24: Use a pipette to aspirate the original culture medium from the culture flask and dispense it into the waste container.
Subtask 25: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM/F12 culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and add it to the culture flask.
Subtask 26: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 27: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 28: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for an additional 2 days.
Subtask 29: Remove the culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the work area.
