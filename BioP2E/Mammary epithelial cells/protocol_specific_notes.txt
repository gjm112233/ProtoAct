1. In Step (1), the required volume of collagenase solution can be calculated from the tissue mass. For approximately 5 g of tissue at 5 mL/g, 25 mL of collagenase solution must be added.
2. Each centrifugation wash with F12 culture medium should include the following operations: place the centrifuge tube in the centrifuge, set the centrifugation parameters, start the centrifuge, remove the centrifuge tube after the specified waiting time, discard the supernatant, add F12 culture medium, and resuspend the cells. After one complete washing cycle, an additional subtask should specify that the corresponding subtasks are repeated until a total of three washes has been completed.
3. After 48 h of culture, the culture dish must be removed from the incubator, the culture medium must be replaced, and the culture dish must then be returned to the incubator. The incubation parameters must be set, and the incubator must be started.
