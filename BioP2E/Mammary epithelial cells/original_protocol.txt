Initial state: Approximately 5 g of minced mouse mammary tissue has been prepared.

(1) Place the minced mammary tissue into an Erlenmeyer flask and add collagenase solution at a volume of 5 mL/g tissue. Place the Erlenmeyer flask in a temperature-controlled shaker and digest at 37°C and 200 rpm for 1 h.

(2) Transfer the digested cell suspension into a centrifuge tube and centrifuge at 800 rpm for 4 min. Discard the supernatant. Wash the cells three times by centrifugation with F12 culture medium, and then resuspend the cell pellet.

(3) Perform cell counting using trypan blue solution.

(4) Dilute the cell suspension with 2× seeding medium to a concentration of (5 \times 10^5) cells/mL. Seed the cells into serum-precoated culture dishes and culture them in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 48 h.

(5) After 48 h, replace the culture medium with growth medium and continue culturing the cells.
