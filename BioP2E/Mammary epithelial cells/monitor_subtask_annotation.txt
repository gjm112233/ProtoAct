Monitor Condition: Approximately 5 g of mouse mammary tissue has been minced into a paste-like consistency. An Erlenmeyer flask, collagenase solution, and a temperature-controlled shaker are available.
Subtask 1: Transfer all the mammary tissue into the Erlenmeyer flask.
Subtask 2: Use a pipette to aspirate 25 mL of collagenase solution and add it to the Erlenmeyer flask.
Subtask 3: Place the Erlenmeyer flask securely in the temperature-controlled shaker.
Subtask 4: Set the temperature-controlled shaker to 200 rpm, 37°C, and 1 h using the control panel.
Subtask 5: Start the temperature-controlled shaker to begin digestion.

Monitor Condition: The temperature-controlled shaker has completed the 1-h operation. A centrifuge tube is available.
Subtask 6: Remove the Erlenmeyer flask from the temperature-controlled shaker and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 7: Use a pipette to transfer all the cell suspension from the Erlenmeyer flask into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 8: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 9: Set the centrifuge to 800 rpm for 4 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 10: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 4-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 11: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 12: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. F12 culture medium is available.
Subtask 13: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of F12 culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 14: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 15: Repeat Subtasks 8–14 three times in total.

Monitor Condition: The cell suspension in the centrifuge tube has been washed three times by centrifugation. Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 16: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 17: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 18: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 19: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the viable cell count and the target concentration of 5 × 10^5 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. A 2× seeding medium and serum-precoated culture dishes are available.
Subtask 20: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of 2× seeding medium and add it to the centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to 5 × 10^5 cells/mL.
Subtask 21: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into a serum-precoated culture dish.
Subtask 22: Place the culture dish securely inside the incubator.
Subtask 23: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 24: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 48 h. Growth medium is available.
Subtask 25: Remove the culture dish from the incubator and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 26: Use a pipette to aspirate the original culture medium from the culture dish and dispense it into the waste container.
Subtask 27: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of growth medium and add it to the culture dish.
Subtask 28: Place the culture dish securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 29: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 30: Start the incubator.
