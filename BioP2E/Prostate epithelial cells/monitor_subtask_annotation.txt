Monitor Condition: Approximately 3 g of mouse prostate tissue has been minced. A 25 cm² culture flask and collagenase solution are available.
Subtask 1: Transfer all the prostate tissue into the 25 cm² culture flask.
Subtask 2: Use a pipette to aspirate 30 mL of collagenase solution and add it to the culture flask.
Subtask 3: Place the culture flask securely in the shaking water bath.
Subtask 4: Set the shaking water bath to 37°C for 1 h using the control panel.
Subtask 5: Start the shaking water bath.

Monitor Condition: The shaking water bath has completed the 1-h operation. A centrifuge tube is available.
Subtask 6: Remove the culture flask from the shaking water bath and place it securely in the workspace.

Subtask 8: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 9: Set the centrifuge to 100 × g for 5 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 10: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 5-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 11: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and secure it in the workspace.
Subtask 12: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. MSS containing 5% serum is available.

Subtask 14: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
