Initial state: MDA-MB-231 cells are cultured in DMEM supplemented with 10% FBS and penicillin–streptomycin.

a. Aspirate the culture medium from the culture dish and rinse the cells with PBS to remove residual medium.

b. Add trypsin and gently shake the dish to distribute it evenly. When the cells become rounded and detach, add culture medium to terminate the digestion.

c. Centrifuge at 800 × g for 6 min and discard the supernatant.

d. Resuspend and dilute the cells in serum-containing culture medium. Seed 1×10^4 cells into each well of a 96-well plate and incubate for 12 h to allow the cells to attach.

e. Discard the culture medium, add 100 μL of the test substance diluted in culture medium to each well, and incubate for 48 h.

f. Add 10 μL of CCK-8 solution to each well and return the plate to the cell culture incubator for an additional 4 h.

g. Measure the absorbance at 450 nm using a microplate reader and calculate cell viability.
