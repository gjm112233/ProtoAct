Monitor Condition: Healthy adherent MDA-MB-231 cells in the logarithmic growth phase are available and cultured in DMEM supplemented with 10% FBS.
Subtask 1: Place the culture dish containing the adherent MDA-MB-231 cells in the work area.
Subtask 2: Use a pipette to slowly aspirate the culture medium from the culture dish and dispense the aspirated medium into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the culture medium has been removed from the culture dish.
Subtask 3: Use the pipette to aspirate 2 mL of PBS and dispense it into the culture dish.
Subtask 4: Gently swirl the culture dish to ensure that the PBS covers all the cells.
Subtask 5: Use the pipette to slowly aspirate the PBS from the culture dish and dispense the aspirated liquid into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the PBS has been removed from the culture dish, and trypsin is available.
Subtask 6: Use the pipette to aspirate 2 mL of trypsin and dispense it into the culture dish.
Subtask 7: Gently swirl the culture dish to distribute the trypsin evenly.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been treated with trypsin for 5 min.
Subtask 8: Move the culture dish to the microscopic observation area.
Subtask 9: Observe the cells in the culture dish using an inverted microscope.

Monitor Condition: Most of the cells have become rounded and detached. DMEM supplemented with 10% FBS and an empty centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 10: Move the culture dish back to the work area.
Subtask 11: Use the pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM supplemented with 10% FBS and dispense it into the culture dish.
Subtask 12: Use the pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the culture dish and transfer the entire suspension into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 13: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 14: Set the centrifuge to 800 × g for 6 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 15: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 6-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 16: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge.
Subtask 17: Use the pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. Cell counting indicates that the centrifuge tube contains X cells. The required volume of serum-containing culture medium has been calculated as V mL, and a 96-well plate is available.
Subtask 18: Use the pipette to aspirate V mL of serum-containing culture medium and dispense it into the centrifuge tube containing the cell pellet.
Subtask 19: Use the pipette to aspirate the liquid from the centrifuge tube and slowly dispense it back into the tube. Repeat this pipetting process 3–5 times to form a uniform cell suspension.
Subtask 20: Use the pipette to aspirate the diluted cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and add 100 μL of the suspension to each well of the 96-well plate.
Subtask 21: Place the 96-well plate securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 22: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 23: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The 96-well plate has been incubated for 12 h.
Subtask 24: Remove the 96-well plate from the incubator and place it in the microscopic observation area.
Subtask 25: Use an inverted microscope to determine whether the cells have attached to the bottom of the wells.

Monitor Condition: Inverted microscopy shows that the cells have settled at the bottom of the wells, spread into a flattened morphology, and attached to the surface. Test substances serially diluted in culture medium are available.
Subtask 26: Move the 96-well plate to the work area.
Subtask 27: Use the pipette to aspirate the culture medium from each well and dispense it into the waste container.
Subtask 28: Replace the pipette tip. Use the pipette to add 100 μL of the test substance at the corresponding concentration to each well of the 96-well plate.
Subtask 29: Place the 96-well plate securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 30: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 31: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The 96-well plate has been incubated for 48 h.
Subtask 32: Remove the 96-well plate from the incubator and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 33: Use the pipette to add 10 μL of CCK-8 solution to each well of the 96-well plate.
Subtask 34: Place the 96-well plate securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 35: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 36: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The 96-well plate has been incubated for an additional 4 h.
