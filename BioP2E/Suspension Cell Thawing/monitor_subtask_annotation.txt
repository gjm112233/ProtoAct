Monitor Condition: The cell-thawing device has been preheated.
Subtask 1: Remove the cryovial from the liquid-nitrogen storage tank, immediately place it in the cell-thawing device.
Subtask 2: Start the cell-thawing device.

Monitor Condition: The cell-thawing cycle has been completed.
Subtask 3: Remove the cryovial from the cell-thawing device and secure it in the workspace.
Subtask 4: Use a pipette to aspirate all liquid from the cryovial and dispense it into a centrifuge tube.
Subtask 5: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor slot of the centrifuge.
Subtask 6: Set the centrifuge to 800 × g for 6 min using the control panel.
Subtask 7: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: Wait until the centrifuge has completed its 6 min run.
Subtask 8: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and secure it in the workspace.
Subtask 9: Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant in the centrifuge tube has been almost completely removed.
Subtask 10: Use a pipette to aspirate 1 mL of serum-containing culture medium and dispense it into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 11: Use a pipette to aspirate the liquid from the centrifuge tube and slowly dispense it back. Repeat this pipetting cycle 3-5 times to resuspend the cells.

Monitor Condition: The cell pellet at the bottom of the centrifuge tube has completely dispersed; a culture dish and serum-containing culture medium have been prepared.
Subtask 12: Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of serum-containing culture medium and dispense it into the culture dish.
Subtask 13: Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and transfer it to the culture dish.
Subtask 14: Gently swirl the culture dish.
Subtask 15: Place the culture dish into the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 16: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO2 concentration to 5% using the control panel.
Subtask 17: Start the incubator.
