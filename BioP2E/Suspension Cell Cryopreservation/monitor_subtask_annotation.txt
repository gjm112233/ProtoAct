Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured in the incubator for 3 days.
Subtask 1: Remove the culture dish from the incubator and place it steadily in the workspace.
Subtask 2: Use a pipette to aspirate all of the cell suspension from the culture dish and transfer it to a centrifuge tube.
Subtask 3: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor slot of the centrifuge.
Subtask 4: Set the centrifuge to 800 × g for 6 min using the control panel.
Subtask 5: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: Wait until the centrifuge has completed its 6 min run.
Subtask 6: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and secure it in the workspace.
Subtask 7: Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant in the centrifuge tube has been almost completely removed. Cell counting has determined that the centrifuge tube contains X cells. The required volume of freezing medium has been calculated as V = X / (1 × 10^6); pre-cooled freezing medium and several cryovials have been prepared.
Subtask 8: Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of freezing medium and dispense it into the centrifuge tube containing the cell pellet.
Subtask 9: Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and dispense 1 mL into each cryovial.
Subtask 10: Place all cryovials in a freezer.
Subtask 11: Set the temperature to -80°C.

Monitor Condition: The cryovials have been stored in the freezer for 12 h.
Subtask 12: Remove all cryovials from the freezer and transfer them to a liquid-nitrogen storage tank.
