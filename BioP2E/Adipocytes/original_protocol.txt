Initial state: Adipose tissue particles cut into approximately 1 mm³ pieces have been prepared.

(1) Place the adipose tissue particles into a centrifuge tube, add an appropriate volume of PBS solution, and centrifuge at 1,000 rpm for 10 min.

(2) Collect the purified adipose tissue particles from the upper layer and gently spread them over the bottom surface of a culture flask. Allow the flask to stand for 5 min. After the adipose tissue particles have firmly adhered, add DMEM culture medium and culture them under standard conditions in an incubator. After 5 days, when numerous short spindle-shaped cells are observed, replace the DMEM culture medium and continue culturing under standard conditions.
