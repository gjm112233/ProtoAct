1. After centrifugation in Step (1), the supernatant should not be discarded because Step (2) requires the adipose tissue particles from the upper layer to be collected and transferred onto the bottom surface of a culture flask.
2. In Step (2), the culture flask must be removed from the incubator and the cells must be observed under a microscope. After microscopic observation, the culture flask must be moved to the workspace. The culture medium must then be replaced, after which the culture flask must be returned to the incubator. The incubation parameters must be set, and the incubator must be started.
