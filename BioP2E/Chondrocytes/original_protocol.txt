Initial state: Cartilage tissue has been cut into pieces of approximately 1 mm³.

(1) Transfer the cartilage tissue pieces into a glass culture dish and add 10 mL of GBSS to completely cover the tissue.

(2) Allow the tissue to stand for 10 min and then remove the GBSS. Add 4 mL of hyaluronidase solution and incubate at room temperature for 5 min. Remove the hyaluronidase solution, add another 8 mL of hyaluronidase solution, and incubate at room temperature for 10 min. Remove the hyaluronidase solution and wash the tissue pieces twice with 5 mL of GBSS per wash. Transfer the tissue pieces into a 25 mL screw-cap tube.

(3) Wash the tissue pieces twice with trypsin, using 2 mL for each wash. Remove the trypsin, add 4 mL of fresh trypsin, and incubate at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 30 min. Remove the trypsin and wash the tissue pieces twice with GBSS, using 5 mL for each wash.

(4) Digest the tissue pieces with 2 mL of 0.2% collagenase solution for 5 min and remove the digestion solution. Add 4 mL of collagenase solution and incubate at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 30 min, then remove the supernatant. Add another 4 mL of collagenase solution and incubate at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 90 min.

(5) Collect the supernatant and centrifuge it at 600 × g for 8 min to obtain a cell pellet.

(6) Resuspend the cells in 8 mL of F12 culture medium containing 20% FBS and centrifuge at 180 × g for 1 min to remove the undigested cartilage matrix.

(7) Transfer the supernatant into another centrifuge tube and centrifuge at 600 × g for 10 min to collect the cells.

(8) Resuspend the cells in 8 mL of F12 culture medium containing 12% FBS and culture them in a 75 cm² culture flask. Add additional culture medium to bring the total volume to 12 mL.
