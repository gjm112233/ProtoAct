Monitor Condition: Split chicken bone shafts, a culture dish, and PBS are available.
Subtask 1: Place the split chicken bone shafts into the culture dish.
Subtask 2: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 20 mL of PBS and add it to the culture dish.
Subtask 3: Gently shake the culture dish.
Subtask 4: Use a pipette to aspirate all the liquid from the culture dish and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 5: Repeat Subtasks 2–4 until a total of three rinses has been completed.

Monitor Condition: Three rinses have been completed. PBS is available.
Subtask 6: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 50 mL of PBS and add it to the culture dish.
Subtask 7: Place the culture dish into the shaking water bath.
Subtask 8: Set the shaking water bath temperature to 37°C and the operating time to 30 min using the control panel.
Subtask 9: Start the shaking water bath.

Monitor Condition: The shaking water bath has completed the 30-min operation. A centrifuge tube is available.
Subtask 10: Remove the culture dish from the shaking water bath and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 11: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the culture dish and transfer all of it into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 12: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 13: Set the centrifuge to 650 × g for 10 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 14: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 10-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 15: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 16: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. PBS and a 50 mL centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 17: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of PBS and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 18: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 19: Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and transfer all of it into the 50 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 20: Place the 50 mL centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 21: Set the centrifuge to 650 × g for 20 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 22: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 20-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 23: Remove the 50 mL centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 24: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the 50 mL centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. PBS is available.
Subtask 25: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 mL of PBS and add it to the 50 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 26: Gently shake the 50 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 27: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the 50 mL centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 28: Repeat Subtasks 25–27 until a total of two rinses has been completed.

Monitor Condition: Two rinses have been completed. PBS is available.
Subtask 29: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 20 mL of PBS and add it to the 50 mL centrifuge tube.

Monitor Condition: Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 30: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 31: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 32: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 33: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the cell-counting results and the target concentration of 1 × 10^6 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. MEM culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and a culture flask are available.
Subtask 34: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of MEM culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and add it to the 50 mL centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to 1 × 10^6 cells/mL.
Subtask 35: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the diluted cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the culture flask.
Subtask 36: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 37: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 38: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 24 h. MEM culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum is available.
Subtask 39: Remove the culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 40: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate all the culture medium from the culture flask and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 41: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of MEM culture medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and add it to the culture flask.
Subtask 42: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 43: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 44: Start the incubator.
