1. Like other laboratory devices, the shaking water bath must include separate operations for setting its parameters and starting the device.
2. After 6 days of culture, the culture flask must be removed from the incubator and placed in the microscopic observation area for observation under a microscope. After the specified cell state is observed, the culture flask must be moved to the work area. The same procedure must be followed after the second culture period.
3. After the second culture period, twice the original volume of RPMI 1640 culture medium containing 10% newborn calf serum must be added. The doubled volume should be 4 mL.
