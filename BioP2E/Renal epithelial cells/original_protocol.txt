Initial state: Washed and homogenized mouse renal cortical tissue has been prepared and placed in a centrifuge tube containing physiological saline.

(1) Collect the renal cortical tissue in a centrifuge tube and pipette thoroughly to disperse the tissue. Centrifuge at 1,000 rpm for 10 min.

(2) After centrifugation, discard the supernatant. Add 2.0 mL of 0.2% trypsin and thoroughly pipette the digestion solution together with the centrifuged tissue. Place the centrifuge tube in a shaking water bath at 37°C and digest for 20 min. Centrifuge at 1,500 rpm for 5 min and discard the supernatant. Add 5 mL of RPMI 1640 culture medium containing 10% newborn calf serum, pipette thoroughly, and transfer the suspension into a culture flask. Culture the cells in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂.

(3) On Day 6 of primary culture, when the cells have completely covered the bottom of the flask, remove the culture medium. Add a small amount of serum-free RPMI 1640 culture medium, gently shake the flask to wet all the cells, and then remove the medium. Add 0.2% trypsin and place the flask in the incubator for 5 min. When approximately 85% of the cells have contracted and become rounded, with a small number of cells detached, immediately add twice the volume of RPMI 1640 culture medium containing 10% newborn calf serum. Gently shake the flask to mix the contents evenly and pipette gently along the bottom of the flask. Centrifuge at 1,500 rpm for 5 min and discard the supernatant. Add RPMI 1640 culture medium containing 10% newborn calf serum and pipette thoroughly to obtain a uniform cell suspension. After cell counting, transfer the cells into culture flasks for continued culture.
