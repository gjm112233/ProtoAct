Monitor Condition: Fetal mouse brain tissue, a sterile 90 mm culture dish, and D-Hanks solution are available.
Subtask 1: Use ophthalmic forceps to place the fetal mouse brain tissue into the sterile 90 mm culture dish.
Subtask 2: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 mL of D-Hanks solution and add it to the 90 mm culture dish.
Subtask 3: Gently shake the 90 mm culture dish.
Subtask 4: Use a pipette to aspirate the D-Hanks solution from the 90 mm culture dish and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: All the D-Hanks solution has been removed from the 90 mm culture dish. Surgical instruments are available.
Subtask 5: Use surgical instruments to cut the brain tissue in the 90 mm culture dish into pieces of approximately 1 mm³.

Monitor Condition: The brain tissue has been cut into pieces of approximately 1 mm³. A centrifuge tube and DMEM/F12 conditioned medium are available.
Subtask 6: Use ophthalmic forceps to transfer the prepared brain tissue pieces into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 7: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 6 mL of DMEM/F12 conditioned medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 8: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.

Monitor Condition: A 200-mesh nylon screen and a new centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 9: Install the 200-mesh nylon screen on the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 10: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the original centrifuge tube.
Subtask 11: Move the pipette above the 200-mesh nylon screen and slowly press the plunger to dispense the cell suspension dropwise onto the nylon screen for filtration.

Monitor Condition: Most of the cell suspension in the original centrifuge tube has been filtered. DMEM/F12 conditioned medium is available.
Subtask 12: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM/F12 conditioned medium.
Subtask 13: Move the pipette above the 200-mesh nylon screen and slowly press the plunger to rinse the inner surface of the screen with the culture medium.

Monitor Condition: All the filtrate has been collected in the centrifuge tube. Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 14: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 15: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 16: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 17: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the cell-counting results and the target concentration of 1 × 10^6 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. DMEM/F12 conditioned medium and a sterile 90 mm culture dish are available.
Subtask 18: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of DMEM/F12 conditioned medium and add it to the centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to 1 × 10^6 cells/mL.
Subtask 19: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of the diluted cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the sterile 90 mm culture dish.
Subtask 20: Place the 90 mm culture dish securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 21: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 22: Start the incubator.
