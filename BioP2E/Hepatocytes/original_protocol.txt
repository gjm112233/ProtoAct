Initial state: Prepared liver tissue fragments are placed in a culture dish.

(1) Wash the liver tissue fragments with D-Hanks solution to remove residual blood. After washing, centrifuge at 800 rpm for 4 min and discard the supernatant. Add Digestion Solution I and incubate at 37°C for 12 min. Wash the liver tissue fragments three times with DMEM to remove the residual trypsin from the digestion solution. Attach the liver tissue fragments to the surface of a 25 cm² culture flask and add a small amount of culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS. Incubate the flask at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 3 h, and then add an additional 6 mL of culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS. Return the flask to the incubator for continued culture. When a growth halo of cells appears around the tissue fragments, replace the medium with culture medium containing 50 mL/L BS and continue incubation at 37°C with 5% CO₂.

(2) When the cells have grown into a monolayer, add Digestion Solution II and digest the cells in an incubator at 4°C for 12 h. Remove the digestion solution, add culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS, and gently pipette the cells to form a cell suspension. Filter the cell suspension through a 200-mesh nylon screen, wash it twice with culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS, and centrifuge at 50 × g for 4 min. Confirm that cell viability is greater than 80% using trypan blue staining. Seed the cells at a density of 5 × 10⁵ cells/mL and culture them at 37°C with 5% CO₂. After the cells have attached, replace the medium with culture medium containing 50 mL/L BS.
