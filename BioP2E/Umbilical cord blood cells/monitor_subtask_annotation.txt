Monitor Condition: A total of 20 mL of umbilical cord blood is available in a 250 mL dropping bottle. A 6% hydroxyethyl starch sodium chloride injection is available.
Subtask 1: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of 6% hydroxyethyl starch sodium chloride injection and add it to the 250 mL dropping bottle.
Subtask 2: Gently shake the 250 mL dropping bottle.

Monitor Condition: The mixture has been allowed to stand for 45 min. A centrifuge tube is available.
Subtask 3: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 3 mL of the cell suspension from the 250 mL dropping bottle and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 4: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 5: Set the centrifuge speed to 1,000 rpm and the operating time to 8 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 6: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 8-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 7: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 8: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. IMDM culture medium is available.
Subtask 9: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of IMDM culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 10: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 11: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 12: Set the centrifuge speed to 1,000 rpm and the operating time to 5 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 13: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 5-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 14: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 15: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. IMDM culture medium is available.
Subtask 16: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of IMDM culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 17: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 18: Repeat Subtasks 11–17 until a total of two centrifugation washes has been completed.

Monitor Condition: Two centrifugation washes have been completed. Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 19: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 20: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 21: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 22: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the cell-counting results and the target concentration of 1 × 10^9 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. IMDM culture medium containing 20% FBS and a 35 mm six-well culture plate are available.
Subtask 23: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of IMDM culture medium containing 20% FBS and add it to the centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to 1 × 10^9 cells/mL.
Subtask 24: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the diluted cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the 35 mm six-well culture plate.
Subtask 25: Place the 35 mm six-well culture plate securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 26: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 27: Start the incubator.
