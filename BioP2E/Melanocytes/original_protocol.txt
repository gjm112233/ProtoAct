Initial state: Skin tissue has been cut into small pieces.

(1) Place the skin tissue into a culture dish containing 0.25% trypsin, with the epidermal surface facing upward. Digest the tissue in a refrigerator at 4°C for 24 h.

(2) Discard the digestion solution and rinse the tissue with D-Hanks solution.

(3) When separation between the epidermis and dermis is observed at the edges of the skin tissue, transfer the tissue into another 10 cm plastic culture dish. Place the tissue with the dermal surface facing downward and rinse it with 5 mL of serum-containing complete culture medium. Gently transfer the epidermal tissue into a 50 mL centrifuge tube, add 20 mL of DMEM culture medium, and pipette repeatedly.

(4) Wash the dispersed cell suspension three times by centrifugation with D-Hanks solution at 2,000 rpm for 5 min each time, discarding the supernatant after each centrifugation.

(5) After the cells have pelleted, resuspend them in melanocyte growth medium and perform cell counting. Seed the cells into a culture flask at a density of (2 \times 10^5) cells/mL and culture them at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 72 h.
