Monitor Condition: Skin tissue cut into small pieces and a culture dish containing 0.25% trypsin are available.
Subtask 1: Transfer the skin tissue pieces into the culture dish containing 0.25% trypsin, with the epidermal surface facing upward.
Subtask 2: Place the culture dish securely inside the refrigerator and close the refrigerator door.
Subtask 3: Set the refrigerator temperature to 4°C using the control panel.

Monitor Condition: The tissue has been digested for 24 h.
Subtask 4: Remove the culture dish from the refrigerator and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 5: Use a pipette to aspirate the 0.25% trypsin digestion solution from the culture dish and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the 0.25% trypsin digestion solution has been removed from the culture dish. D-Hanks' solution is available.
Subtask 6: Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of D-Hanks' solution and add it to the culture dish to cover the skin tissue.
Subtask 7: Gently shake the culture dish.
Subtask 8: Use a pipette to aspirate the D-Hanks' solution from the culture dish and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 9: Move the culture dish to the microscopic observation area and observe the skin tissue in the culture dish using an inverted microscope.

Monitor Condition: Separation between the epidermis and dermis is observed at the edges of the skin tissue. A new 10 cm plastic culture dish and serum-containing complete culture medium are available.
Subtask 10: Use forceps to pick up the separated skin tissue pieces from the culture dish and transfer them into the new 10 cm plastic culture dish, with the dermal surface facing downward against the bottom of the dish.
Subtask 11: Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of serum-containing complete culture medium and add it to the plastic culture dish.
Subtask 12: Gently shake the plastic culture dish.
Subtask 13: Use a pipette to aspirate the serum-containing complete culture medium from the culture dish and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the serum-containing complete culture medium has been removed from the plastic culture dish. A 50 mL centrifuge tube and DMEM culture medium are available.
Subtask 14: Use bent forceps to grasp the edge of the epidermis and gently peel off the entire epidermal layer along the separation interface, then place the epidermal tissue into the 50 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 15: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 20 mL of DMEM culture medium and add it to the 50 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 16: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 10–15 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 17: Place the 50 mL centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 18: Set the centrifuge to 2,000 rpm for 5 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 19: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The 5-min centrifugation process has been completed.
Subtask 20: Remove the 50 mL centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 21: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the 50 mL centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. D-Hanks' solution is available.
Subtask 22: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 20 mL of D-Hanks' solution and add it to the 50 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 23: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the 50 mL centrifuge tube and gently pipette 3–5 times to resuspend the cells.
Subtask 24: Repeat Subtasks 17–23 until a total of three centrifugation washes has been completed.

Monitor Condition: Three centrifugation washes have been completed. Melanocyte growth medium is available.
Subtask 25: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the 50 mL centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 26: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 20 mL of melanocyte growth medium and add it to the 50 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 27: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the 50 mL centrifuge tube and gently pipette 3–5 times to resuspend the cells.

Monitor Condition: Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 28: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 29: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 30: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 31: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the viable cell count and the target concentration of 2 × 10^5 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. Melanocyte growth medium and a culture flask are available.
Subtask 32: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of melanocyte growth medium and add it to the 50 mL centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to 2 × 10^5 cells/mL.
Subtask 33: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of the diluted cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the culture flask.
Subtask 34: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 35: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 36: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 72 h.
Subtask 37: Remove the culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the workspace.
