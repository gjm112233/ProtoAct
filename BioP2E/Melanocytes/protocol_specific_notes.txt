1. In Step (2), rinsing with D-Hanks solution should include the following operations: add D-Hanks solution, gently shake the culture dish, and remove the solution. After rinsing, the cells must be observed under a microscope.
2. In Step (3), rinsing with 5 mL of serum-containing complete culture medium should include the following operations: add the serum-containing complete culture medium, gently shake the culture dish, and remove the medium.
3. In Step (4), each centrifugation wash should include the following operations: centrifuge the cell suspension, discard the supernatant, add Hanks solution, and resuspend the cells. A volume of 20 mL of Hanks solution should be added during each wash. After one complete washing cycle, an additional subtask should specify that the corresponding subtasks are repeated until a total of three washes has been completed.
4. After the three centrifugation washes, the supernatant must be discarded once more to remove the remaining Hanks solution. Then, 20 mL of melanocyte growth medium should be added to resuspend the cells.
5. After 72 h of culture in Step (5), an operation for removing the culture flask from the incubator must be included.
