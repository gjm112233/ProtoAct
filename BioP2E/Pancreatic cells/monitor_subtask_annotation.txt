Monitor Condition: A thoroughly cleaned pancreas, surgical tools, a 25 mL conical flask, and cell isolation solution are available.
Subtask 1: Use surgical tools to cut the pancreas into pieces of approximately 1 mm³.
Subtask 2: Use ophthalmic forceps to transfer the pancreatic tissue pieces into the 25 mL conical flask.
Subtask 3: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of cell isolation solution and add it to the 25 mL conical flask.
Subtask 4: Place the 25 mL conical flask on the shaker.
Subtask 5: Set the shaker temperature to 37°C, the rotational speed to 120 rpm, and the operating time to 15 min using the shaker control panel.
Subtask 6: Start the shaker.

Monitor Condition: The shaker has completed the 15-min operation. A 40 μm nylon strainer and a centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 7: Remove the 25 mL conical flask from the shaker and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 8: Install the 40 μm nylon strainer on the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 9: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the 25 mL conical flask.
Subtask 10: Move the pipette above the 40 μm nylon strainer and slowly press the plunger to dispense the cell suspension dropwise onto the strainer for filtration.

Monitor Condition: Most of the cell suspension in the conical flask has been filtered. Cell isolation solution is available.
Subtask 11: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of cell isolation solution.
Subtask 12: Move the pipette above the 40 μm nylon strainer and slowly press the plunger to rinse the inner surface of the strainer with the cell isolation solution.

Monitor Condition: All the filtrate has been collected in the centrifuge tube. A 20 μm nylon strainer and a 40 mL centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 13: Install the 20 μm nylon strainer on the 40 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 14: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 15: Move the pipette above the 20 μm nylon strainer and slowly press the plunger to dispense the cell suspension dropwise onto the strainer for filtration.

Monitor Condition: Most of the cell suspension in the centrifuge tube has been filtered. Cell isolation solution is available.
Subtask 16: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of cell isolation solution.
Subtask 17: Move the pipette above the 20 μm nylon strainer and slowly press the plunger to rinse the inner surface of the strainer with the cell isolation solution.

Monitor Condition: All the filtrate has been collected in the 40 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 18: Place the 40 mL centrifuge tube into the designated rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 19: Set the centrifuge force to 250 × g and the operating time to 5 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 20: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 5-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 21: Remove the 40 mL centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 22: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the 40 mL centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant has been removed from the 40 mL centrifuge tube. HBSS-DVC and 4% BSA are available.
Subtask 23: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 mL of HBSS-DVC and add it to the 40 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 24: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the 40 mL centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 25: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of 4% BSA and add it to the 40 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 26: Place the 40 mL centrifuge tube into the designated rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 27: Set the centrifuge force to 100 × g and the operating time to 5 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 28: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 5-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 29: Remove the 40 mL centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 30: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the 40 mL centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant has been removed from the 40 mL centrifuge tube. F12 culture medium and a 25 mL culture flask are available.
Subtask 31: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 mL of F12 culture medium and add it to the 40 mL centrifuge tube.
Subtask 32: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the 40 mL centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 33: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate all the cell suspension from the 40 mL centrifuge tube and transfer it into the 25 mL culture flask.
Subtask 34: Place the 25 mL culture flask securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 35: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 36: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 12 h.
Subtask 37: Remove the 25 mL culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the workspace.

Monitor Condition: The culture flask has been allowed to stand for 5 h. F12 culture medium is available.
Subtask 38: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the existing culture medium from the 25 mL culture flask and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 39: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 mL of F12 culture medium and add it to the 25 mL culture flask.
Subtask 40: Place the 25 mL culture flask securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 41: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 42: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 48 h.
Subtask 43: Remove the 25 mL culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the workspace.
