Initial state: A thoroughly cleaned pancreas has been obtained.

(1) Cut the pancreas into pieces of approximately 1 mm³. Transfer the tissue pieces into a 25 mL conical flask and add 5 mL of cell isolation solution. Agitate the flask on a shaker at 37°C and 120 rpm for 15 min.

(2) Filter the cell suspension in the conical flask through a 40 μm nylon strainer. After filtration, rinse the nylon strainer with cell isolation solution.

(3) Filter the cell suspension collected in the centrifuge tube again through a 20 μm nylon strainer, and collect the filtrate in a 40 mL centrifuge tube. After filtration, rinse the nylon strainer with cell isolation solution.

(4) Centrifuge at 250 × g for 5 min and discard the supernatant. Resuspend the cells in 10 mL of HBSS-DVC, add 5 mL of 4% BSA, centrifuge at 100 × g for 5 min, and remove the supernatant.

(5) Add 10 mL of F12 culture medium, transfer the cell suspension into a 25 mL culture flask, and culture it in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 12 h.

(6) After culture, remove the culture flask from the incubator and allow it to stand for 5 h. Discard the culture medium, add 10 mL of F12 culture medium, and return the culture flask to the incubator for an additional 48 h of culture.
