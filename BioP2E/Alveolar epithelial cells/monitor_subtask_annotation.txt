Monitor Condition: The washed and minced mouse lung tissue is placed in a beaker containing 4 mL of DNase. Fetal bovine serum, Alveolar Lavage Solution II, and a flask are available.
Subtask 1: Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of fetal bovine serum and add it to the beaker.
Subtask 2: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 20 mL of Alveolar Lavage Solution II and add it to the beaker.
Subtask 3: Use a pipette to transfer all the liquid and tissue from the beaker into the flask.
Subtask 4: Place the flask securely in the shaking water bath.
Subtask 5: Set the shaking water bath to 37°C for 5 min using the control panel.
Subtask 6: Start the shaking water bath.

Monitor Condition: The shaking water bath has completed the 5-min operation. A 200-mesh nylon screen and a centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 7: Remove the flask from the shaking water bath.
Subtask 8: Install the 200-mesh nylon screen on the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 9: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the flask.
Subtask 10: Move the pipette above the 200-mesh nylon screen and slowly press the plunger to dispense the cell suspension dropwise onto the nylon screen for filtration.

Monitor Condition: Most of the liquid in the flask has been filtered. DMEM culture medium is available.
Subtask 11: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM culture medium and dispense it dropwise onto the inner surface of the 200-mesh nylon screen for washing.

Monitor Condition: Most of the cell suspension in the flask has been filtered, and the filtered cell suspension has been collected in the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 12: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 13: Set the centrifuge to 400 × g for 5 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 14: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifugation has been completed after 5 min.
Subtask 15: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 16: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. A culture flask containing DMEM culture medium is available.
Subtask 17: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of serum-free DMEM culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 18: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 19: Use a pipette to transfer all the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube into the culture flask containing DMEM.
Subtask 20: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator.
Subtask 21: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 22: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 3 h. A centrifuge tube is available.
Subtask 23: Remove the culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 24: Use a pipette to transfer all the cell suspension from the culture flask into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 25: Place the centrifuge tube into a rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 26: Set the centrifuge to 400 × g for 10 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 27: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifugation has been completed after 10 min.
Subtask 28: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 29: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. Serum-free DMEM culture medium is available.
Subtask 30: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of serum-free DMEM culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 31: Gently shake the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 32: Use a pipette to aspirate the culture medium from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the waste container.

Monitor Condition: Most of the liquid has been removed from the centrifuge tube. DMEM culture medium containing 20% fetal bovine serum is available.
Subtask 33: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM culture medium containing 20% fetal bovine serum and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 34: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.

Monitor Condition: Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 35: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 36: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 37: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 38: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the counting results, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. A culture flask and DMEM culture medium containing 20% fetal bovine serum are available.
Subtask 39: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of RPMI 1640 culture medium containing 20% newborn calf serum and add it to the centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to the specified concentration.
Subtask 40: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate an appropriate volume of the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the culture flask.
Subtask 41: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator.
Subtask 42: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 43: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 24 h. DMEM culture medium containing 20% fetal bovine serum is available.
Subtask 44: Remove the culture flask from the incubator and place it securely in the work area.
Subtask 45: Use a pipette to aspirate the original culture medium from the culture flask and dispense it into the waste container.
Subtask 46: Replace the pipette tip. Aspirate 2 mL of DMEM culture medium containing 20% fetal bovine serum and add it to the culture flask.
Subtask 47: Place the culture flask securely inside the incubator.
Subtask 48: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 49: Start the incubator.
