Initial state: A rabbit lymph node has been obtained.

(1) Transfer the lymph node into a culture dish containing HBSS and cut it into pieces of approximately 1 mm³. Place the culture dish in a shaking water bath and shake at 37°C for 20 min.

(2) Remove the culture dish from the shaking water bath and repeatedly pipette the tissue pieces. Filter out the tissue pieces using a 100-mesh strainer and collect the filtrate. Wash the cells twice by centrifugation at 1,000 rpm for 10 min, using 5 mL of DMEM culture medium for each wash.

(3) Transfer the cell suspension into a culture dish and resuspend the pelleted cells in 2 mL of DMEM culture medium. Place the culture dish in an incubator and culture at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 30 min.

(4) Aspirate the existing culture medium, add 5 mL of DMEM culture medium, and centrifuge at 1,000 rpm for 10 min.

(5) Resuspend the pelleted cells in 5 mL of DMEM culture medium. Count the cells and adjust the cell density to 1×10^6 cells/mL. Seed the cells into a culture dish and culture them in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 48 h.
