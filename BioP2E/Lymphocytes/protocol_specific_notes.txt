1. In Step (1), a culture dish containing HBSS is already available. The lymph node should therefore be transferred directly into this culture dish.
2. In Step (1), the tissue-cutting operation should be parsed as: use surgical tools to cut the lymph node into pieces of approximately 1 mm³. After cutting, a monitor condition should confirm that the lymph node has been completely cut into pieces before the culture dish is placed in the shaking water bath.
3. In Step (2), after filtration through the 100-mesh strainer, the inner surface of the strainer must be rinsed.
4. In Step (2), the cells must be washed twice by centrifugation. Each washing cycle should include the following operations: centrifuge the cell suspension, discard the supernatant, add 5 mL of DMEM culture medium, and resuspend the cells. After one complete washing cycle, an additional subtask should specify that the corresponding subtasks are repeated until a total of two centrifugation washes has been completed.
5. After 30 min of culture, the culture medium must be replaced, followed by an implicit operation that transfers the cell suspension into a centrifuge tube.
6. The supernatant must be discarded after every centrifugation operation.
7. At the end of Step (5), after 48 h of culture, the culture dish must be removed from the incubator.
