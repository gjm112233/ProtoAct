Monitor Condition: A rabbit lymph node, a culture dish containing HBSS, and surgical tools are available.
Subtask 1: Use ophthalmic forceps to transfer the rabbit lymph node into the culture dish containing HBSS.
Subtask 2: Use surgical tools to cut the rabbit lymph node into pieces of approximately 1 mm³.

Monitor Condition: The lymph node has been cut into pieces of approximately 1 mm³.
Subtask 3: Place the culture dish into the shaking water bath.
Subtask 4: Set the shaking water bath temperature to 37°C and the operating time to 20 min using the control panel.
Subtask 5: Start the shaking water bath.

Monitor Condition: The shaking water bath has completed the 20-min operation.
Subtask 6: Remove the culture dish from the shaking water bath and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 7: Replace the pipette tip. Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times.

Monitor Condition: A 100-mesh strainer and a centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 8: Install the 100-mesh strainer on the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 9: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the cell suspension from the culture dish.
Subtask 10: Move the pipette above the 100-mesh strainer and slowly press the plunger to dispense the cell suspension dropwise onto the strainer for filtration.

Monitor Condition: Most of the cell suspension in the culture dish has been filtered. DMEM culture medium is available.
Subtask 11: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM culture medium.
Subtask 12: Move the pipette above the 100-mesh strainer and slowly press the plunger to rinse the inner surface of the strainer with the culture medium.

Monitor Condition: All the filtrate has been collected in the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 13: Place the centrifuge tube into the designated rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 14: Set the centrifuge speed to 1,000 rpm and the operating time to 10 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 15: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 10-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 16: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 17: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. DMEM culture medium is available.
Subtask 18: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of DMEM culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 19: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 20: Repeat Subtasks 13–19 until a total of two centrifugation washes has been completed.

Monitor Condition: Two centrifugation washes have been completed. A culture dish and DMEM culture medium are available.
Subtask 21: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to transfer all the cell suspension from the centrifuge tube into the culture dish.
Subtask 22: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of DMEM culture medium and add it to the culture dish.
Subtask 23: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the culture dish, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.
Subtask 24: Place the culture dish securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 25: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 26: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 30 min. DMEM culture medium and a centrifuge tube are available.
Subtask 27: Remove the culture dish from the incubator and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 28: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate all the culture medium from the culture dish and dispense it into the liquid waste container.
Subtask 29: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of DMEM culture medium and add it to the culture dish.
Subtask 30: Use a pipette to transfer all the cell suspension from the culture dish into the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 31: Place the centrifuge tube into the designated rotor position in the centrifuge.
Subtask 32: Set the centrifuge speed to 1,000 rpm and the operating time to 10 min using the centrifuge control panel.
Subtask 33: Start the centrifuge.

Monitor Condition: The centrifuge has completed the 10-min centrifugation process.
Subtask 34: Remove the centrifuge tube from the centrifuge and place it securely in the workspace.
Subtask 35: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate the supernatant from the centrifuge tube and dispense it into the liquid waste container.

Monitor Condition: The supernatant has been removed from the centrifuge tube. DMEM culture medium is available.
Subtask 36: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 5 mL of DMEM culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube.
Subtask 37: Insert the pipette tip below the liquid surface in the centrifuge tube, slowly press and release the plunger, and repeat the pipetting process 3–5 times to resuspend the cell suspension.

Monitor Condition: Trypan blue staining solution, a hemocytometer, and an empty test tube are available.
Subtask 38: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the cell suspension and add it to the empty test tube.
Subtask 39: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of trypan blue staining solution and add it to the same test tube.
Subtask 40: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 10 μL of the mixture from the test tube and add it to the counting chamber of the hemocytometer.
Subtask 41: Place the hemocytometer under a microscope, observe and count the numbers of viable and dead cells, and calculate the proportion of viable cells.

Monitor Condition: Based on the cell-counting results and the target concentration of 1 × 10^6 cells/mL, the required volume of additional culture medium has been calculated as V mL. DMEM culture medium and a culture dish are available.
Subtask 42: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate V mL of DMEM culture medium and add it to the centrifuge tube to dilute the cell suspension to 1 × 10^6 cells/mL.
Subtask 43: Replace the pipette tip. Use a pipette to aspirate 2 mL of the diluted cell suspension from the centrifuge tube and seed it into the culture dish.
Subtask 44: Place the culture dish securely inside the incubator and close the incubator door.
Subtask 45: Set the incubator temperature to 37°C and the CO₂ concentration to 5% using the incubator control panel.
Subtask 46: Start the incubator.

Monitor Condition: The cells have been cultured for 48 h.
Subtask 47: Remove the culture dish from the incubator and place it securely in the workspace.
