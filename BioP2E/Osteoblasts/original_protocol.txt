Initial state: Bone fragments cut into pieces of approximately 2 mm³ have been obtained.

(1) Place the bone fragments into a culture dish and wash them with 10 mL of culture medium containing fetal bovine serum. After washing, add 10 mL of osteoblast digestion solution and digest at room temperature for 45 min.

(2) Discard the cell suspension obtained from the first digestion. Add another 10 mL of osteoblast digestion solution and digest at room temperature for 30 min. Collect the digestion solution and centrifuge at 580 × g for 2 min.

(3) Remove the supernatant and resuspend the cells in 8 mL of Ham’s F12 culture medium containing 20% fetal bovine serum. Centrifuge at 580 × g for 10 min, discard the supernatant, and resuspend the cells in 4 mL of complete culture medium.

(4) Seed 4 mL of the cell suspension into a 75 cm² culture flask, add 8 mL of complete culture medium, and culture in an incubator at 37°C with 5% CO₂ for 20 min.

(5) Discard the culture medium and add 2 mL of complete culture medium. Then add 4 mL of cell suspension, followed by 6 mL of complete culture medium. Return the culture flask to the incubator and continue culturing.
