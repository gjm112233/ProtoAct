(2) When the cells have grown into a monolayer, add Digestion Solution II and incubate at 4°C for 12 h. Remove the digestion solution, add culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS, and gently pipette to form a cell suspension. Filter the suspension through a 200-mesh nylon screen, wash twice with culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS, and centrifuge at 50 × g for 4 min. Confirm that the viable-cell percentage determined by trypan blue staining is greater than 80%, seed the cells at a density of 5 × 10^5 cells/mL, and culture them at 37°C with 5% CO2. After the cells have attached, replace the medium with culture medium containing 50 mL/L BS.
