Hepatocyte Step 1:
Cut liver tissue fragments have been prepared and placed in a culture dish.
(1) Wash the residual blood from the liver tissue fragments with D-Hanks solution. After washing, centrifuge at 800 r/min for 4 min, discard the supernatant, add Digestion Solution I, and incubate at 37°C for 12 min. Then wash three times with DMEM culture medium to remove the trypsin remaining in the digestion solution. Attach the liver tissue fragments to a 25 cm² culture flask, add a small amount of culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS, and incubate at 37°C with 5% CO2 for 3 h. Then add another 6 mL of culture medium containing 100 mL/L BS and continue incubation. After a cell outgrowth halo appears around the tissue fragments, replace the medium with culture medium containing 50 mL/L BS and continue incubation at 37°C with 5% CO2.
